I am a third year PhD student at the University of Western Australia, and a member of the [OzGrav](https://www.ozgrav.org) collaboration. I am supervised by Fiona Panther (UWA), and Evgeni Grishin (Monash). My current research studies the dynamics of stellar mass black holes inside the accretion discs of active galactic nuclei (AGN): how they migrate, lock into mean motion resonances, and eventually merge to produce gravitational waves. I am interested in what gravitational wave detectors from LIGO to LISA can learn from such mergers, including detecting signatures of the environment, testing general relativity, and producing alternative methods of measuring Hubble's constant.

My work combines regularised few-body simulations with post-Newtonian corrections and analytic resonance theory borrowed from planetary dynamics. I will complete my PhD in 2027 and am applying for postdoctoral positions starting in late 2027. My [CV](/cv/) and [publications](/publications/) are linked above.
